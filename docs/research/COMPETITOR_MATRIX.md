# Competitor Matrix

- **Phase:** P1 research (REQ-030).
- **Retrieval dates:** 2026-10-20 (all sources below unless noted).
- **Method:** public pages only. No accounts were created, no uploads, no payments, no scraping beyond ordinary page retrieval. No competitor was tested with the same inputs, so this is **not** a performance benchmark.
- **Label key:** `VERIFIED` = directly observed on the cited public page; `PUBLIC CLAIM` = the vendor states it publicly but BuildScope has not tested it; `INFERENCE` = analyst reading based on observed facts; `UNKNOWN` = not observable publicly.

## Observations that shape BuildScope (INFERENCE, not independently verified performance)

1. **Bid comparison is crowded, but "compare by scope" is not the same as "judge against a market target".** RemodelerIQ and EstimateHawk lean on market pricing or scoring. BuildScope can differentiate by preserving per-document evidence and by classifying silence as `NOT SPECIFIED` rather than pricing it silently.
2. **Materials-package and owner-supply workflows appear weakly covered** by the public positioning of all three. Owner-builders and kit buyers are a stated BuildScope target (REQ-012).
3. **Change-order economics are rarely shown publicly as a first-class delta with an unknown-fee callout.** BuildScope treats requested, quoted, approved and rejected changes separately.
4. **Free contractor-funded comparison is an established alternative.** BuildScope's one-time paid model must justify itself with evidence quality and report usability, not with claims of superiority.
5. **Public claims are not implementation proof.** All capability rows above remain `PUBLIC CLAIM` or `UNKNOWN` until tested with representative inputs or public demonstrations.
